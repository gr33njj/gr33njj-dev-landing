## Context

Healthcare is a domain where mistakes carry a real cost.
The `telemedicine` project required a careful and structured approach.

## Key challenges

- complex entities and relationships
- reliability requirements
- long-term extensibility

## Approach

I started with modeling, not code.
Until the system structure was clear, writing business logic made no sense.

## What matters here

In projects like this:
- development speed is secondary
- clarity beats feature count
- simplicity is a rare luxury

## Outcome

The project became a strong exercise in system thinking and working with responsibility-heavy domains.
